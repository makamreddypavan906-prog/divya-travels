Popular destinations

Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh

A historic Rayalaseema town known for Besant Theosophical College. Rabindranath Tagore visited the college in 1919, where Jana Gana Mana was translated into English.

  • Besant Theosophical College
  • Chitralaya Museum
  • Local markets and regional food
Madanapalle town history ↗

Horsley Hills, Andhra Pradesh

A peaceful hill station in the Eastern Ghats, famous for cool weather, green slopes, eucalyptus trees and wide valley views.

  • Windy Rocks viewpoint
  • Scenic hill drives and nature walks
  • Forest views and photography
Horsley Hills travel guide ↗

Tirumala, Andhra Pradesh

A major pilgrimage destination in the Seven Hills, home to the revered Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple.

  • Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple
  • Akasa Ganga and Papavinasanam
  • Swami Pushkarini and Sila Thoranam
Official TTD website ↗

Sompalle, Andhra Pradesh

A village in Mulakalacheruvu mandal, Annamayya district, known for the historic Sri Chennakesava Swamy Temple and its stone architecture.

  • Sri Chennakesava Swamy Temple
  • Historic stone pillars and temple carvings
  • Traditional village and temple atmosphere
